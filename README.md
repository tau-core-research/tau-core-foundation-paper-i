# Tau Core Foundation Paper I

Private foundation-paper repository:

**Tau Core: An Atemporal Morphological Readout Framework and a Conditional
Minimum-Viable Physical Completion**

The manuscript is self-contained and does not use another Tau Core paper,
repository, or unpublished analysis as evidence for its claims.

## Main Claim

The paper defines a body/readout architecture in which observed physical
descriptions are observer-indexed descents of an endpoint-blind parent
response:

```text
s -> R_tau(s) -> M_tau -> Desc_O(M_tau).
```

It then freezes one conditional minimum-viable completion:

```text
Gamma_MVP -> M_* -> Desc_O -> {GR/optics, quantum/Born, QOR bound}.
```

Inside that completion, the GR/optics and quantum/Born standard leaves are
compatible and a morphology-dependent single-record resolution inequality is
derived. Its quantum-speed-limit step is stated through a standard
Fubini--Study/Bures state angle; inside the adopted common-Gram completion,
Uhlmann minimum lift proves the morphology-to-distance conversion. The
Tau-specific discriminator is the independently reconstructed same-source
identity between the body-action ratio and the Uhlmann chord, not an extra
timing delay. Nature-level occupation, laboratory realization and empirical
validation remain open. Relative to the current source axioms, physical record
occurrence and the finite body--Uhlmann identity are proved logically
independent; an operational QOR trial requires both together with a separately
verified record-hold condition.

The manuscript also identifies a finite sufficient source theorem for the
body--Uhlmann identification. If the global self-adjoint Dirac operator
is affiliated with the von Neumann algebra generated by the source-owned
recovery relations, its central recovery projectors reduce the operator and
one fixed spectral action is exactly additive across the recovery blocks.
An explicit source-owned block connector transfers this spectral direct sum
to orthogonal Uhlmann-amplitude blocks in one common action unit; only the two
ingredients together yield finite A8b without an extra gain. Spectral
additivity alone is insufficient. The direct-sum amplitude form assumes a
central superselection sector without inter-block coherence; coherent
off-block amplitudes require a richer completion. A two-block countermodel
proves that recovery support and faithful
occupation alone do not imply affiliation. Inside the declared source-minimal
primary-SHR completion, however, the reconstructed seed boundary and pure
Hodge-isometric realization generate the affiliated Hodge--Dirac operator.
Any non-isometric alternative requires additional primitive base-work rank and
is outside the MVP. Nature-level occupation of the primary-SHR/source-minimal
premises remains open.

The connector itself is now derived conditionally rather than left as an
unstructured map. If one complete finite central source algebra supplies both
the spectral blocks and the Hilbert--Schmidt standard-form state carrier,
recovery completeness and continuous orthogonal additivity reduce each finite
action law to `c_alpha d_U,alpha^2`; the inherited common action unit fixes
`c_alpha=1`. The selected connector is
`Phi_alpha=[sqrt(rho_alpha)]`, unique up to right-unitary gauge. The physical
parent's occupation of this common finite source law remains open.

That occupation question is now reduced to one exact finite law. A single
represented normalized trace fixes the faithful central weights. If the
parent response is the quotient-basic squared Hilbert--Schmidt Trace--Gram
distance, the full connector packet follows; conversely this response is
unique among continuous, chord-only, orthogonally additive laws with the
inherited unit. The explicit control `Y+lambda Y^2` preserves the same local
Hessian and source data but violates finite composition, so physical
Trace--Gram occupation is not claimed.

The final nature-level claim is explicitly non-identifiable from the evidence
currently assembled in the paper. The manuscript defines a six-part `FOC-6`
certificate requiring a held single-shot record, independent same-carrier
action and Uhlmann reconstructions at multiple nonzero separations, downstream
timing, and standard/nonlinear controls. No candidate platform has completed
this certificate; internal closure is not presented as empirical occupation.

For finite source algebras, the manuscript also gives an exact conditional
selection theorem. A complete source-owned algebra, faithful state, and typed
role packet determine the minimal cyclic completion up to typed unitary
equivalence by the GNS construction. A separate countermodel proves that even
complete terminal-sector marginals do not determine that joint packet: the
new four-sector theorem shows that even all proper marginals leave 81 genuine
joint directions unresolved. One onto source incidence with positive
stiffness is sufficient for a faithful joint Gram state, and the remaining
finite selection problem compresses to one coherent physical
`*-homomorphism`, not an arbitrary list of cross-sector gains.

The record-occurrence side is also sharper. In an enriched dagger-Frobenius
base completion, the record-copy map is the metric adjoint of primitive
multiplication and follows as the unique minimum of one positive unfolding
functional, without a fitted record gain. The narrow parent action has not
been shown to own this two-leg completion, so physical occupation remains
open.

## Does Not Claim

- It does not claim empirical validation of Tau Core.
- It does not derive the adopted completion uniquely from the physical
  base--seed source.
- Conditional Einstein and Born recovery is not an unconditional derivation
  of GR or quantum mechanics from the minimal Tau base.
- It does not supply ultraviolet quantum gravity, full interacting QFT, the
  Standard Model, numerical fundamental constants, or LCDM replacement.
- It does not replace dark matter, dark energy, or general relativity.
- It does not import numerical or synthetic results from other Tau Core work as
  evidence for the framework.
- It does not present a nature-selected final parent action or a complete
  validated physical theory.
- It does not claim that any candidate laboratory platform is already a Tau
  carrier or that the parent predicts a nonzero carrier effect size.

## Scope

The English v0.3 manuscript is the canonical reviewer-facing source.  The
Hungarian manuscript currently remains a legacy v0.2 translation and is not
claim-equivalent to the English v0.3 paper.

This repository is a foundation-paper / conditional-completion package.  Its
job is to make the Tau Core parent/body/readout architecture and its strongest
finite completion discussable without smuggling conditional recovery into a
claim of physical selection.
The current manuscript further proves that physically occupied base--seed
response stabilized before observer descent belongs to the complete
morphological body. A partial-body defect is therefore a truncation diagnostic.
Under universal-seed order completeness, decoder-vertical chain mismatch is
removed only when it is a true action/terminal null, and the affiliated
Hodge--Dirac/A8b construction extends to faithful rich bodies without requiring
minimum body dimension.
