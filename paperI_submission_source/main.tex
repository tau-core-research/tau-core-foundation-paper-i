Inside a special dagger-Frobenius record completion, a complete source-owned record edge induces exact Trace--Gram endpoint transport and composable path holonomy. As the architectural entry point of the seven-stage Roman Foundation Series (Papers I--VII-C), this foundational manuscript does not claim empirical validation; unrestricted parent-law realization, laboratory occupation, arbitrary-state coverage, and ultraviolet/Standard-Model completion remain open.

currently frozen physical completion in a form that can be criticized,
reproduced, and rejected.  It is self-contained: no result from another Tau

\begin{proposition}[Unified terminal atlas and common-ancestor criterion]
\label{prop:mopr-ancestor}
Fix an occupied body and observer--source context, and let
\(\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}:\mathcal X_{OS}\to\mathcal Z_{OS}\) be the source-owned shared
preterminal descriptor supplied by the body-conditioned full channel.  For the
declared terminal-role set \(\mathcal A_{\rm term}\), the unified readout is the
typed product map
\begin{equation}
 \mathfrak R_{OS}
 =
 \left\langle
   Q_{OS}^{(a)}\circ T_{OS}^{(a)}
 \right\rangle_{a\in\mathcal A_{\rm term}}
 \circ\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\circ A_{OS}[M_\tau],
 \qquad
 M_\tau=\operatorname{Stab}_{B_\tau^{\rm base}}(\Sigma_\tau).
 \label{eq:unified-terminal-atlas}
\end{equation}
The last equality is the adopted source-class stabilization law, not an
unrestricted Nature-level theorem.  A candidate terminal
\(R:\mathcal X_{OS}\to\mathcal Y\) descends through the same immediate ancestor
if and only if it is constant on its fibres:
\begin{equation}
 \Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}(x)=\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}(x')
 \Longrightarrow R(x)=R(x').
 \label{eq:mopr-fibre-criterion}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, there is a unique
\(\bar R:\operatorname{im}\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\to\mathcal Y\) with
\(R=\bar R\circ\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\).  On a regular smooth branch with connected fibres,
the local criterion is
\begin{equation}
 \ker D\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\subseteq\ker DR.
 \label{eq:mopr-kernel-criterion}
\end{equation}
The declared terminal stack recovers the same local quotient as the ancestor
exactly when
\begin{equation}
 \ker D\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}
 =
 \ker
 \begin{bmatrix}
  DR^{(1)}\\ \vdots\\ DR^{(n)}
 \end{bmatrix}.
 \label{eq:mopr-stack-completeness}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
If \(R=\bar R\circ\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\), equality of descriptor values implies equality
of terminal values.  Conversely, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mopr-fibre-criterion} makes
\(\bar R(\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}(x)):=R(x)\) well defined and unique on the image.  The
differential statement follows from the constant-rank theorem on connected
local fibres.  Equality in Eq.~\eqref{eq:mopr-stack-completeness} says that
the stack erases exactly, rather than strictly more than, the tangent
directions erased by the ancestor.
\end{proof}

This theorem owns only the conditional common ancestry of the declared,
physically occupied atlas.  It does not prove ambient terminal exhaustivity.
The control \(\Xi(x,y)=x\), \(R_1(x,y)=x\), \(R_2(x,y)=y\) shows why:
\(R_1\) factors and \(R_2\) does not.  A physically occupied terminal with
\(D\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}v=0\) but \(DRv\neq0\) would therefore falsify completeness of the
proposed common ancestor rather than authorize a fitted repair.

\begin{corollary}[Exact legacy migration through the continuous descriptor]
\label{cor:mopr-migration}
Let $L:\mathcal X_{OS}\to\mathcal Y_L$ be a historical descriptor or
terminal law.  There is a unique map
$U:\operatorname{im}\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\to\mathcal Y_L$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 L=U\circ\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}
 \label{eq:mopr-legacy-migration}
\end{equation}
if and only if every \(\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\)-fibre is contained in an
$L$-fibre.  The two descriptors are exactly equivalent on the occupied
domain if and only if their fibre equivalence relations coincide.  On a
regular smooth branch the necessary local test is
$\ker D\Xi_{OS}^{\rm cont}\subseteq\ker DL$, with equality giving the same
local quotient.  Equal rank is not sufficient: the rank-one maps
$\Xi(x,y)=x$ and $L(x,y)=x+y$ fail Eq.~\eqref{eq:mopr-legacy-migration}.
\end{corollary}

This migration theorem acts before hard finite resolution.  The operational
record is separately
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
and a hard quantizer migrates only when its stable partition is compatible
with the same fibre map.  The corollary transports an already supplied
terminal law or calibration; it does not derive the physical
$Q_{OS,\delta}$, any calibration, $q_R(R)$, or Nature occupation.

\begin{lemma}[Local metric of the common-Gram minimum lift]
Assume constant rank in a neighborhood of \(q_0\) and choose a smooth local
horizontal gauge.  Let \(W(q)\) be a twice-differentiable normalized amplitude
curve through \(W(q_0)\),
and let \(\dot W_h\) be its horizontal tangent after minimizing the vertical
right-unitary direction.  Then
\begin{equation}
 \min_U\lVert W(q_0+\delta q)-W(q_0)U\rVert_{\rm HS}^2
 =\delta q^2\lVert\dot W_h\rVert_{\rm HS}^2+O(|\delta q|^3).
 \label{eq:minimum-lift-second-variation}
\end{equation}
Under A8b, the pullback of this quotient metric is
\(G_O/\mathcal A_*\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write a nearby gauge representative as
\(W(q_0+\delta q)U(\delta q)^\dagger
=W(q_0)+\delta q(\dot W-W(q_0)X)+O(\delta q^2)\), with
\(X^\dagger=-X\).  Minimizing the quadratic coefficient over \(X\) removes the
vertical tangent and leaves \(\dot W_h\).  Squaring the Hilbert--Schmidt norm
gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:minimum-lift-second-variation}.  A8b identifies this
horizontal quotient norm, with the common action unit restored, with the
body-Hessian pullback in Eq.~\eqref{eq:observer-pullback-metric}.  The last
identification is therefore explicit completion structure, not a consequence
of Hessian positivity alone.
\end{proof}

 \label{eq:gram-bures-identity}

declared self-adjoint unitary protocol.  Open or noisy evolution requires its

\section{Mixed-state distance and QFI conventions}
\label{app:bures-qfi}

To avoid the common squared-versus-root fidelity ambiguity, this manuscript
uses the \emph{squared} Uhlmann fidelity
\begin{equation}
  F(\rho,\sigma)
  =\left[\operatorname{Tr}
  \sqrt{\sqrt\rho\,\sigma\sqrt\rho}\right]^2,
  \qquad 0\leq F\leq1.
  \label{eq:appendix-squared-fidelity}
\end{equation}
The root fidelity is
\(f(\rho,\sigma)=\sqrt{F(\rho,\sigma)}\).  The Bures angle used in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:quantum-record-angle} is therefore
\begin{equation}
  D_B(\rho,\sigma)=\arccos f(\rho,\sigma)
  =\arccos\sqrt{F(\rho,\sigma)}.
  \label{eq:appendix-bures-angle}
\end{equation}
For pure states, \(F=|\langle\psi|\varphi\rangle|^2\), so
\(D_B=\arccos|\langle\psi|\varphi\rangle|\), the Fubini--Study angle.

For a differentiable one-parameter mixed-state path, the infinitesimal Bures
line element and quantum Fisher information \(\mathcal F_Q\) satisfy
\begin{equation}
  ds_B^2=\frac14\mathcal F_Q(t)\,dt^2.
  \label{eq:appendix-bures-qfi}
\end{equation}
For unitary evolution generated by a self-adjoint \(H\),
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal F_Q(t)\leq\frac{4\Delta H(t)^2}{\hbar^2},
  \qquad
  \frac{ds_B}{dt}\leq\frac{\Delta H(t)}{\hbar},
  \label{eq:appendix-unitary-qfi-bound}
\end{equation}
which integrates to Eq.~\eqref{eq:state-distance-speed-bound}
\cite{Taddei2013}.  A nonunitary channel must instead use its actual QFI path
speed; Eq.~\eqref{eq:appendix-unitary-qfi-bound} is not assumed for it.
