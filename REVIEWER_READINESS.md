## Scientific Claim Boundary

The paper contains two claim levels:

1. A weak atemporal parent/body/readout architecture with source-freeze,
   quotient-safety, and no-double-counting rules.
2. One conditional minimum-viable completion at the level of a common
   variational packet.

The completion conditionally supplies:

- a semiclassical Einstein/optics standard leaf;
- a fixed or classical-dynamic-geometry quantum/Born standard leaf;
- compatibility of their classical limits;
- the exact QOR single-record inequality
  `delta t_min,O >= 2 hbar arcsin(epsilon_O/2) / Delta H`, whose conservative
  linear corollary is `hbar epsilon_O / Delta H`.

Neither the inverse-energy dependence nor the standard Uhlmann
chord-to-angle conversion is claimed as new. The candidate new content is the
same-source identity x_A = A_X^PC/A_* = x_Q = 2(1-sqrt(F)), reconstructed
independently by a closed body-action curvature/work protocol and state
tomography. Once that identity is imposed, the timing inequality is the
standard Bures-angle quantum speed limit, not an additional Tau delay. A
distinctive positive test therefore requires a source-side prediction of x_A
before tomography or timing. An
ordering of measured onset thresholds follows only under independently
established saturation or equal-slack conditions.

The QOR derivation now uses an explicit Fubini--Study/Bures state angle rather
than an undefined action difference. In the adopted common-Gram completion,
the action ratio is the global right-unitary Hilbert--Schmidt minimum-lift
distance of normalized record amplitudes. Uhlmann minimization proves
`epsilon_O^2 = 2(1-sqrt(F)) = 4 sin^2(D_B/2)` and therefore
`D_B = 2 arcsin(epsilon_O/2) >= epsilon_O`. The former A9 distance bridge is
thus a theorem of the completion rather than a separate assumption.

The full qubit state carrier is `D(C^2)`; `CP^1` is only the pure-state
projective submanifold used by the prototype. The pullback stiffness is given
an endpoint-blind operational estimator: an independently fixed closed
calibration protocol measures the symmetric curvature of an unwrapped action
phase with respect to a source-declared carrier coordinate. State tomography
then determines `D_Q`, while independent energy statistics determine
`Delta H`. When the calibration curve is the restriction of the same stationary
body action, the chain rule proves that its curvature equals the parent-Hessian
pullback. These identities can be tested before the timing endpoint is opened.
Physical occupation and laboratory realization of the common-Gram carrier
remain open.

The global minimum lift already includes the full purification fiber and hence
removes the ambient-state-space shortcut problem inside this completion.
Pointwise metric domination alone would remain insufficient for any alternative
completion lacking that global quotient identity.

Assumption A8 is now split into a proved kinematic part and two physically
open occupation/selection inputs. The support-restricted theorem proves that
one nonzero recovery incidence, restricted to its occupied support, together
with one source-owned CP record instrument produces a common Uhlmann-amplitude
carrier. Ambient surjectivity and uniqueness of the global stationary branch
are not required for this theorem. A8a-OCC is only the physical occupation of
that support/instrument packet. A8b separately identifies the horizontal
Hilbert--Schmidt pullback with the body-action metric without a free scale.
Unequal minimal purification supports are covered by the equivalent
partial-isometry form; infinite-dimensional extensions require additional
domain control.
The local lemma is restricted to a constant-rank neighborhood with a smooth
horizontal gauge, and the displayed square-space optimization uses a two-sided
unitary condition.

The record domain itself is no longer an untyped apparatus placeholder. A
positive effect admits a finite two-state Naimark pointer and the canonical
square-root CP instrument, while a positive matching functional stabilizes any
supplied finite record incidence. What remains open is physical occurrence:
whether the parent occupies at least one observer--record incidence. Positive
record Hessian does not imply a nonzero occurrence and does not supply a
finite observer-time hold interval.

The empirical audit is explicitly staged: same-body curvature calibration,
then the endpoint-free Gram/Bures identity test from tomography, and only then
the timing experiment. Candidate platforms are listed only as outlook
possibilities, not as physically identified Tau carriers.
The one-record event is operationally separated from estimation: one run must
enter a predeclared pointer decision region below an error ceiling and remain
there for a fixed hold time. Tomography, averaging, and repeated trials are
calibration or estimator procedures, not the constrained event itself.

The A8b source question is reduced to two distinct defects. Source isometry is
E_src = C^* h C - K_*, while observer-access loss is the positive operator
Delta_O = C^* h(I-P_O)C. Zero observer loss does not prove source isometry.
A8b follows from the normalized same-action connector identities
C^*hC=K_* and P_OC=C. An explicit mixed-state qubit realization exists for
every 0<x_O<=2 and is compatible with CP observer restriction. The weaker
source laws admit a continuous family of loss strengths, so they do not select
the required physical Stinespring incidence V_phys=V_r(x_O).

The canonical manuscript now goes further inside its declared MVP. The
primary-SHR seed possibility family reconstructs an oriented order complex;
pure quotient descent and action basicness force a Hodge-isometric chain map.
The induced nonzero nilpotent recovery differential generates an affiliated
Hodge--Dirac operator. Any positive failure of Hodge isometry factors through
an independently occupied base-work carrier whose minimum dimension equals
the defect rank. Because A4-SRCMIN admits no separately typed rank-novel
base-work role, the MVP forces zero defect and derives the affiliated
Hodge--Dirac recovery structure. Finite A8b additionally requires the explicit
source-owned block connector; it is not a consequence of affiliation or
spectral additivity alone. Nature-level occupation of those premises remains
open.

The manuscript also retains the more general exact sufficient source theorem
for A8b. If the
global self-adjoint Dirac operator is affiliated with the von Neumann algebra
generated by the source-owned recovery relations, the central recovery
projectors strongly commute with it, the operator decomposes as their direct
sum, and one fixed spectral trace is finitely additive over the blocks. A
separate lemma now identifies the exact transfer map: source-owned connectors
from each spectral block to an orthogonal horizontal amplitude block, one
common action unit, a block-preserving Uhlmann gauge, local source isometry,
and the finite block endpoint identity. These premises yield
`epsilon_O^2=sum_alpha epsilon_alpha^2` without a new gain.
The direct-sum amplitude assumption is now typed as central superselection:
off-central-block coherences belong to a richer completion not covered by the
lemma. The manuscript also freezes the next source-theorem target
`(A_s,omega_s,Pi_phys) -> ({Z_alpha},A_*,{Phi_alpha},{U_alpha})` and states that
local source isometry does not imply finite endpoint rigidity. An affine
horizontal lift with a globally consistent blockwise Uhlmann minimizer is
listed only as a strong sufficient route, not as a derived property.
The manuscript now also proves a conditional finite-source selection theorem:
for one complete central finite algebra in Hilbert--Schmidt standard form,
recovery completeness and continuous orthogonal additivity force the finite
law to be linear in the squared Uhlmann chord, while the inherited common
action unit fixes its coefficient to one. Hence
`Phi_alpha=[sqrt(rho_alpha)]` is source-selected up to right-unitary gauge in
that class. This moves the open question upstream to physical occupation of
the common finite source law; it does not turn the result into an unconditional
nature-level derivation.
The remaining occupation question is also finite rather than an unrestricted
connector search. The manuscript proves that one represented normalized trace
plus a quotient-basic squared Hilbert--Schmidt Trace--Gram parent response is
necessary and sufficient within the declared continuous, chord-only,
orthogonally compositional, common-unit class. A `Y+lambda Y^2` counterfamily
retains the same algebra, state, local Hessian and unit while breaking finite
composition. Thus the exact unresolved physical claim is occupation of the
Trace--Gram law, not mathematical availability or uniqueness of the connector.
The terminal nature-level statement is also an identifiability boundary:
current evidence is equally compatible with occupied and nonoccupied parent
models sharing the displayed standard limits and local data. The explicit
`FOC-6` certificate states what must be reconstructed independently on one
carrier before physical occupation can be promoted. No candidate platform in
the outlook has yet passed that certificate.
An explicit two-block counterpair shows that recovery cyclicity, central support, and
faithful occupation alone do not imply affiliation. The current parent has
not yet been shown to construct its Dirac operator from this algebra, so the
result is conditional rather than nature-level selection. The new primary-SHR
proposition constructs the affiliated operator instead of assuming it, but
only in the declared minimum-source completion.

The rich-body extension removes minimum carrier dimension as a hidden A8b
premise. Every occupied base--seed response stabilized before descent is typed
as part of the complete body. The full block-Hessian pullback has zero
realization defect under action basicness. This classification is explicitly
endpoint-blind: observed endpoints, empirical residuals, fitted terminal
coefficients, and desired limits cannot define body membership. Injectivity
constructs a unique decoder only on the realized image. A1-USC additionally
assumes a source-owned, order-intertwining extension on the smallest occupied
chain-closed subspace; no canonical extension to the full body space is
claimed. That extension confines any raw chain mismatch to vertical redundancy;
complete action/terminal
invisibility makes it a true null, while a visible decoder-null ordering
component falsifies universal-seed completeness. The resulting physical
quotient inherits the nilpotent recovery incidence and affiliated Hodge--Dirac
operator. Finite A8b still requires the separately stated block connector.
For the finite rich-body realization the complete recovery algebra is now
defined explicitly as the unital star algebra generated by the descended
incidence, its adjoint, and any remaining source-owned recovery incidences.
Consequently `N_hat,N_hat^dagger in A_rec` and
`D_hat=N_hat+N_hat^dagger in A_rec subset A_rec''`; finite-dimensional
membership is stronger than affiliation. Chain-degree projectors and central
recovery projectors are kept distinct: the former grade the complex, whereas
the latter are degree-preserving central reducers and commute with the
incidence generators.

The manuscript proves a two-way independence result relative to the current
source axioms. Record occurrence does not imply the finite A8b identity:
the same occupied pointer structure admits Stinespring connectors with
different Uhlmann chords. Conversely, the A8b carrier identity does not imply
that a record incidence is physically occupied. All four truth assignments
are realized by a finite qubit family. A realized QOR trial therefore requires
the conjunction of record occurrence, finite A8b calibration, and an
independently declared hold condition. A richer source law may select these
jointly, but neither can be used as proof of the other.

The joint-source audit is now stronger than a marginal counterexample. In a
four-qubit control, local plus pairwise Pauli words span 66 of 255 traceless
joint directions, while even all proper-subsystem words span only 174. The
faithful family `(I + epsilon sigma_z^tensor4)/16` preserves every proper
marginal and changes a genuine four-sector moment. The remaining 81 dimensions
are precisely the genuine four-body Pauli subspace in this four-qubit
decomposition; 81 is not asserted as a representation-independent count for
arbitrary four-sector models. Conversely, one
source-owned onto incidence with positive stiffness produces a faithful joint
Gram state and fixes all mixed moments. For the once-owned P1--P4 role ledger,
the remaining ideal/representation choice reduces to one coherent unital
`*-homomorphism` from the primitive role colimit. Its physical selection is
open.

The finite GNS statement is correspondingly restricted. It compares two
minimal cyclic realizations of the same fixed abstract source algebra, faithful
state, and typed role identifications. The unique intertwiner is unique on the
cyclic span once it maps one cyclic vector to the other and implements that
fixed algebra identification. This is representation uniqueness after the
source packet is supplied, not a theorem that the current Tau parent selects
the packet. Unquotiented source-algebra automorphisms are not silently counted
as physical equivalences.

A separate source theorem sharpens A8a-OCC. In an enriched metric
dagger-Frobenius base, the copy map `Delta=mu^dagger` is fixed by primitive
multiplication, and the positive functional
`A_unfold=||y-mu^dagger x||^2/2` has the unique minimum
`y=mu^dagger x`. This removes a free record gain inside that completion. It
does not show that the current narrow parent action owns the required two-leg
carrier or occupies its nonzero branch. At fixed occupied record roots and
resolution, closing each root under body, causal, clock, and record incidence
gives a unique inclusion-minimal primitive observer atlas; it does not predict
the number of physically occupied observers.

A once-counted effective-action ledger is now explicit. It separates the
pre-body solve, a microscopic ownership partition, and the renormalized
observer-indexed effective functional. It also fixes counterterm relocation,
Green/Hessian ownership, and exact Schur elimination. This proves the
effective and quadratic bookkeeping once microscopic ownership and a
renormalization prescription are supplied; it does not supply the nonlinear
interacting microscopic continuum functional. The manuscript also records a carrier no-go: ordinary ground
energy curvature and the Fubini--Study metric have different spectral
weights. A constant-gap rotating qubit has quantum metric `1/4` but zero
ground-energy curvature, so a generic energy-Hessian calibration cannot prove
A8b.

For a classical dynamic metric, the Born state/effect form survives
conditionally in the locally covariant algebraic formulation. A quantized
metric would require a constrained joint geometry--matter state/effect
construction that Tau Core does not yet possess.

The former extended `R^3` hidden-defect section has been reduced to a compact
toy witness. The manuscript now also exposes three residual risks directly:
the microscopic ownership partition is not parent-derived; the Born leaf on a
quantized metric still needs a constrained joint geometry--matter algebra; and
the semiclassical gravity leaf is not validated in strong-fluctuation or
black-hole evaporation regimes.

## Hard Non-Claims

The manuscript does not claim:

- nature-level selection of the completion from the physical base and seed;
- an unconditional derivation of GR or quantum mechanics from minimal Tau;
- ultraviolet or nonperturbative quantum gravity;
- a complete interacting QFT or measurement ontology;
- Standard Model, particle-spectrum, or numerical-constant prediction;
- a universal time atom;
- dark-matter or dark-energy replacement;
- empirical confirmation of the QOR discriminator or Tau ontology.
