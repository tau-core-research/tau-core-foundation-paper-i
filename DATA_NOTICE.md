# Data Notice

This foundation paper contains no empirical dataset and makes no endpoint
validation claim.  It states a conditional observer-resolution lower-bound
discriminator but does not report a test of that discriminator.  The repository includes only
manuscript source, generated conceptual figures, and smoke-test artifacts.

The figures are schematic diagrams generated from local scripts.  They are not
measurements and should not be interpreted as empirical evidence for Tau Core.
